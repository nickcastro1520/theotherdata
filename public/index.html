How it works

1

Collect

A scheduled job pulls fresh numbers from public sources like the St. Louis Fed, the SEC, the FDA, the TSA, Wikipedia, and the GDELT news index. No logins, no paywalls, no scraping where it isn't allowed.

2

Compare

Each signal is compared with its own recent past (last 28 days vs the 28 before, or this year vs last year). Small moves inside a noise band are called neutral. See one signal, start to finish →

3

Explain

Every card says what the data is, why it might matter for that company, and what it's showing right now, written from the actual numbers. If a source fails, we say so instead of guessing.
